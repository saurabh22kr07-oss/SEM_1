💡 Frequently Asked Questions

How are quiz scores calculated?

Each correct question awards 1 point. There is currently no negative marking, so give every question a try!

Can I retake a quiz?

Yes! You can retake any quiz at any time. All attempts are saved in your personalized Results History.

What happens if the timer runs out?

Your quiz will be automatically submitted with whatever answers you have selected so far.
